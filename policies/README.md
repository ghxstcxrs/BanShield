# Manual Roblox ToS and policy bundle

Use roblox_tos_and_policies.template.txt, save a completed UTF-8 copy as roblox_tos_and_policies.txt in this folder, then reload the policy file in BanShield's Text evidence analysis. You can also import a completed .txt file through the app. All five sections are required: terms, standards, appeals, moderation, support. Each requires its official HTTPS Source URL, Retrieved date, Effective date or unknown, and official text after Content:.

The template links to the exact Help Center pages and lists EU/DSA clauses to preserve. Copy the complete pages, not just the checklist or selected favorable lines. Do not omit ordinary appeal restrictions or exceptions. The application verifies format only; it cannot certify copied text completeness or authenticity. The AI is instructed to assess EU eligibility rather than apply EU-specific rights to everyone, and to flag historical policy-version uncertainty and legal enforceability as separate questions.

Earlier roblox_tos.md files and templates are retained but no longer loaded. To migrate, copy their terms, standards, appeals and support sections into the new template and manually append the official Content Moderation page as moderation. Recheck all sources and dates. Automatic fetching remains disabled.
