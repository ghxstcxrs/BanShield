# Manually collected Roblox ToS and policies

Copy this template to roblox_tos_and_policies.txt in this folder. Paste the complete official texts into all five sections, retaining headings, exceptions, geographic scope and deadlines. Replace Retrieved with the actual YYYY-MM-DD collection date; Effective is a published YYYY-MM-DD date or unknown. These files are manually prepared; BanShield performs no policy downloads.

EU/DSA completeness checklist for your copied Appeals and Content Moderation pages:
- Support Form pathway when a violation is not shown in Violations & Appeals.
- General appeal deadline, review/duplicate restrictions, and account-owner requirements.
- Additional EU rights: six-month appeal window after the initial moderation decision.
- EU access to certified out-of-court dispute settlement bodies and any linked official list.
- Applicable qualifications, report categories, and judicial-redress language when present.
Do not replace the official text with this checklist. Applicability and legal enforceability require separate review.
